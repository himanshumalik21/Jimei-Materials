High-Frequency Electromagnetic Physics

Minimizing Skin-Depth Conductor Loss at Millimeter-Wave Frequencies

At 40 GHz, the electromagnetic skin depth in electroplated copper shrinks to approximately 0.33 µm. On standard thick-film or rough DBC ceramic substrates where surface roughness ($R_a$) exceeds 0.5 µm to 1.0 µm, high-frequency current is forced to travel along the irregular microscopic contours of the copper-ceramic interface. This artificially increases the effective conductor path length, creating catastrophic insertion loss, phase distortion, and signal attenuation.

Jimei Materials addresses this by utilizing double-side polished ceramic blanks with surface roughness $R_a < 0.05 ext{ \mu m}$. Sputtered titanium/copper seed layers combined with photolithographic pattern electroplating produce straight-walled conductor traces with pristine edge acuity, enabling verified high-frequency RF circuit layouts up to 40 GHz.

50Ω Microstrip Precision

Photolithographic definition achieves ±5 µm line width accuracy, maintaining strict 50Ω ±2Ω characteristic impedance across high-frequency transmission lines, couplers, and power dividers.

Buried HTCC RF Feedthroughs

For hermetic packages, HTCC technology co-fires internal 50Ω stripline feedthroughs surrounded by ground planes, providing seamless RF transition across hermetic package walls without open microstrip radiation.

High Heat Dissipation for GaN RF PAs

GaN-on-SiC and GaN-on-diamond RF power amplifiers generate extreme localized heat densities. AlN DPC substrates (≥170 W/m·K) provide direct conductive heat removal while maintaining high dielectric isolation.

DFM Guidelines for 40 GHz RF Layouts

  • Thin Gold Plating for RF: Specify flash gold (≤0.05 µm) on top of nickel to minimize insertion loss caused by resistive nickel skin-effect penetration at >10 GHz.
  • Ground Via Pitch: Place RF ground vias at intervals <λ/10 to suppress substrate resonant modes and cavity oscillations.
  • Coplanar Waveguide Gap: Maintain minimum 35 µm ground-to-signal gap with ±5 µm tolerance.
  • Laser Cavities for MMICs: Laser-milled cavities allow drop-in MMIC die placement with coplanar wire bond lengths <0.25 mm.
  • Substrate Thickness: 0.25 mm or 0.38 mm substrates recommended to push dielectric waveguide cutoff modes beyond 40 GHz.
  • Edge Chamfers: Diced perimeter edges chamfered to prevent RF surface wave radiation.
