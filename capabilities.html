Platform 04

Bare Technical Ceramics Machining & Polishing

High-purity partner-sintered raw ceramic blanks with complete in-house secondary laser processing, grinding, and polishing.

Full Bare Ceramic Profile

Machining Tolerances & Dimensions

  • Dimensional Span: Custom square, rectangular, and round substrates from 1.0 mm × 1.0 mm up to 150 mm × 150 mm (6" wafer format).
  • Laser Cutting Tolerance: ±0.05 mm contour outline tolerance; laser kerf width 0.08–0.12 mm.
  • Laser Hole Drilling: Hole diameters down to 0.05 mm (50 µm) with positional accuracy of ±0.02 mm.
  • Substrate Thickness: 0.15 mm, 0.20 mm, 0.25 mm, 0.38 mm, 0.50 mm, 0.635 mm, 1.0 mm, and custom grind thicknesses.
  • Monthly Capacity: Up to 500,000 bare ceramic substrate units per month.

Surface Finishes & Surface Roughness

  • As-Fired Surface: Ra 0.3 µm – 0.6 µm (standard for DBC bonding and general thermal isolation).
  • Lapped Surface: Ra 0.1 µm – 0.2 µm (consistent thickness control for fine-pitch assembly).
  • Double-Sided Polished: Ra < 0.05 µm (optical-grade finish mandatory for 20 µm DPC photolithography and thin-film PVD).
  • Camber & Flatness: ≤0.003 mm/mm (≤0.3%) standard; precision lapping achieves ≤0.001 mm/mm.
  • Edge Chamfering: Micro-chamfering and corner radiusing to prevent edge chipping during automated pick-and-place.
