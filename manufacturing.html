Step-by-Step Production

Complete In-House Ceramic Metallization & Fabrication Process Flow

Documented visual record of core production stages conducted across our Shenzhen and Zhaoqing manufacturing facilities.

STEP 01

Substrate Lapping & Polishing

Incoming Al₂O₃, AlN, or Si₃N₄ bare ceramic plates from qualified technical ceramic partners undergo ultrasonic cleaning, precision surface lapping, and double-side polishing to achieve surface roughness (Ra) down to <0.05 µm prior to metallization.

STEP 02

Laser Micro-Via Drilling

High-precision UV/CO₂ laser drilling creates blind vias and through-holes (down to 50 µm diameter) with minimal taper and zero ceramic micro-cracks.

STEP 03

Vacuum Sputtering (PVD)

Magnetron physical vapor deposition deposits a nanometer-scale Titanium adhesion layer followed by a high-purity Copper seed layer under high vacuum.

STEP 04

Cleanroom Photolithography

Conducted in Class 1,000 cleanrooms using dry-film photoresist and precision laser direct imaging (LDI) or photomasks to achieve 20 µm line/space resolution.

STEP 05

Pattern Electroplating

High-speed acid copper plating lines build dense, void-free copper traces from 10 µm up to 100+ µm while simultaneously filling micro-vias for solid thermal vias.

STEP 06

Resist Strip & Differential Etch

Chemical stripping removes the sacrificial photoresist, followed by micro-etching to strip the Ti/Cu seed layer with sharp sidewall profiles and no undercut.

STEP 07

Surface Finishing (ENIG / ENEPIG)

Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold (ENIG), ENEPIG (Ni/Pd/Au), Immersion Silver, or Direct Solder Coating applied for wire bonding (Au/Al) and solder wetting.

STEP 08

Laser Profiling & Singulation

Picosecond and nanosecond laser dicing machines profile complex outline geometry, edge notches, cavities, and scribe-and-break lines with ±20 µm positioning accuracy.

STEP 09

100% Electrical Testing (E-Test)

Automated flying-probe and dedicated bed-of-nails testers verify 100% netlist electrical continuity, open/short detection, and dielectric insulation resistance.

STEP 10

AOI & Final Quality Assurance

High-resolution Automated Optical Inspection (AOI), X-ray fluorescence (XRF) coating thickness metrology, and pull/shear adhesion testers ensure MIL/IPC compliance.

STEP 11

Cleanroom Vacuum Packaging

Substrates are ultrasonically cleaned, desiccated, and sealed in anti-static vacuum-nitrogen pouches with humidity indicator cards for safe international air transport.
