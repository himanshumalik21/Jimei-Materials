HTCC Manufacturing Process Principles

HTCC packaging utilizes "green tape" (unfired ceramic tape casting made of high-purity Al₂O₃ or AlN powder with organic binders and plasticizers). The process enables true three-dimensional circuitry:

  1. Via Punching & Cavity Routing: High-speed mechanical punches or lasers form 100–150 µm interconnection vias and multi-tiered chip cavities in green tape sheets.
  2. Refractory Screen Printing: Tungsten (W) or Molybdenum (Mo) conductor pastes are screen-printed to fill vias and print routing traces on each individual layer.
  3. Collation & Lamination: Multiple printed tape layers are precisely aligned under optical cameras and warm-isostatically laminated under high pressure.
  4. High-Temperature Co-Firing: The monolithic stack is fired at 1500°C – 1600°C in a controlled reducing hydrogen/nitrogen atmosphere, sintering ceramic and refractory metal into a single solid ceramic block.
  5. Post-Metallization & Brazing: Exposed tungsten/molybdenum pads are electroplated with Ni/Au, followed by high-vacuum brazing (Ag-Cu eutectic) of Kovar seal rings, pins, and heat sinks.
