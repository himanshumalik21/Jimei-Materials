High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic (HTCC) provides monolithic, three-dimensional ceramic packaging engineered for hermetic protection in harsh operational environments. Co-firing green ceramic tape with refractory tungsten and molybdenum conductors at 1500°C – 1600°C creates seamless, rugged enclosures with internal circuit routing, multi-tiered cavities, buried feedthroughs, and brazed seal rings for high-reliability electronics.

Ceramic Co-Firing

HTCC Manufacturing Process Principles

HTCC packaging utilizes unfired green ceramic tape cast from high-purity Al₂O₃ powder with organic binders and plasticizers. This enables true three-dimensional monolithic circuitry with internal conductors, cavities, and hermetic seals:

  1. Via Punching & Cavity Routing: Precision mechanical punches and lasers form interlayer connection vias and multi-tiered chip cavities in individual green tape sheets.
  2. Refractory Screen Printing: High-purity Tungsten (W) or Molybdenum (Mo) conductor pastes are screen-printed to fill vias and print circuit traces on each internal layer.
  3. Collation & Lamination: Multiple printed tape layers are optically aligned and laminated under heat and isostatic pressure into a monolithic green ceramic block.
  4. High-Temperature Co-Firing: The monolithic stack is fired at 1500°C – 1600°C in a controlled reducing hydrogen/nitrogen atmosphere, sintering ceramic and refractory conductors simultaneously into a dense, hermetic structure.
  5. Post-Metallization & Eutectic Brazing: Exposed refractory pads are electroplated with Ni/Au, followed by high-temperature vacuum brazing (Ag-Cu eutectic) of Kovar seal rings, metal leadframes, and feedthrough pins.

High-Reliability Packaging

HTCC Applications: Multilayer, Hermetic & Harsh Environments

Where standard packaging and organic materials degrade due to moisture ingress, thermal extremes, or mechanical vibration, HTCC delivers impermeable, monolithic ceramic enclosures engineered for mission-critical reliability:

Hermetic Packages & Microelectronic Enclosures
Impermeable Environmental Isolation

Monolithic ceramic shells featuring brazed Kovar seal rings designed for parallel seam welding or laser welding of metal lids. Provides an absolute barrier against atmospheric moisture, corrosive gases, and pressure differentials.

  • Verified hermeticity with Helium leak rate <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s.
  • Eliminates internal condensation and corrosion for hybrid microelectronics, crystals, and oscillators.
RF & Microwave Hermetic Packages
Buried Stripline Feedthroughs & Shielding

High-frequency ceramic packaging utilizing buried stripline and microstrip feedthroughs to route microwave signals into sealed cavities beneath brazed Kovar rings without impedance disruption or seal degradation.

  • Internal ground and power planes provide isolation and EMI/RFI shielding.
  • Supports monolithic microwave integrated circuits (MMICs), power amplifiers, and T/R modules.
Sensor Packages & Optical MEMS Enclosures
Multi-Tier Recessed Cavities

Three-dimensional stepped cavities created prior to co-firing allow recessed mounting of silicon MEMS sensors, infrared detectors, pressure transducers, and photodetector chips.

  • Brazed window lids (sapphire, quartz, germanium) or sealed headers protect delicate sensor membranes.
  • Low thermal expansion matching silicon minimizes sensor mechanical stress and zero-offset drift.
High-Reliability & Harsh-Environment Electronics
Extreme Operating Temperature & Vibration

Refractory tungsten metallization co-fired at 1500°C–1600°C creates an inorganic, heat-resistant monolithic structure that does not melt, outgas, or delaminate under harsh conditions.

  • Engineered for aerospace, avionics, energy exploration, and industrial downhole telemetry.
  • Resistant to severe thermal shock cycles, mechanical vibration, and corrosive industrial chemical exposure.
