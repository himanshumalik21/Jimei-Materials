Solid-State Refrigeration Physics

Mechanical Uniformity & Thermal Cycling in Thermoelectric Modules

In thermoelectric modules, dozens or hundreds of alternating P-type and N-type semiconductor pellets are soldered between two parallel ceramic plates. When electrical current flows, heat is pumped from the cold ceramic plate to the hot ceramic plate via the Peltier effect. This creates a severe continuous temperature differential ($\Delta T = 60^\circ ext{C}$ to $100^\circ ext{C}+$) across a gap of just 1.0 to 2.0 mm.

Any microscopic variation in substrate thickness or surface camber concentrates mechanical clamping stress onto individual brittle pellets, causing premature pellet cracking and module failure. Jimei Materials guarantees thickness tolerances of ±0.015 mm via precision double-sided lapping, ensuring uniform mechanical load distribution and durable thermal cycling endurance.

High-Density Copper Tab Arrays

Class 1,000 cleanroom photolithography patterns alternating copper interconnect pads with inter-pellet spacing down to 0.10 mm, eliminating solder bridging risks in miniature micro-TEC modules.

Anti-Diffusion Barrier Plating

Dense nickel barrier layers (3–7 µm) electroplated prior to immersion gold or tin prevent bismuth telluride solder components from diffusing into the copper, preventing intermetallic embrittlement.

Precision Lapped Parallelism

Simultaneous double-sided planetary lapping controls top-to-bottom plate parallelism within 0.010 mm, maximizing heat pumping efficiency between hot and cold module heat sinks.

DFM Guidelines for Thermoelectric Substrates

  • Pellet Pad Sizing: Design copper pads 0.05 mm larger per side than the bismuth telluride pellet cross-section for solder fillets.
  • Lead Wire Pads: Provide enlarged perimeter copper pads for soldering external stranded wire leads.
  • Solder Resist Mask: Optional high-temperature solder mask dam between pads to constrain low-temp solder flow.
  • Edge Clearance: Keep metallized copper tabs ≥0.30 mm away from outer diced substrate edges.
  • Center Holes: Laser-drilled center apertures supported for optical beam pass-through in laser cooling applications.
  • Multi-Stage Cascades: Scribed stepped substrates supported for 2-stage and 3-stage pyramid TEC modules.
Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Direct answers to common fabrication, material selection, and layout questions.

Standard Alumina (96% Al₂O₃) substrates (24 W/m·K) are widely used in commercial consumer TECs. However, in high-power laser diode cooling and optical transceiver micro-coolers where heat flux exceeds 20 W/cm², the thermal resistance of alumina plates causes thermal bottlenecks. Switching to AlN (≥170 W/m·K) reduces temperature drops across the ceramic plates by over 85%, significantly increasing maximum coefficient of performance (COP) and maximum achievable $\Delta T_{max}$.

Our copper metallization with nickel/gold or pure tin finish is compatible with low-temperature bismuth-tin (BiSn, 138°C), indium-tin (InSn, 118°C), and standard lead-free SAC305 (217°C) alloys commonly used during thermoelectric pellet array reflow.

Unlike thick-film printed pastes that suffer from glass frit degradation under repetitive expansion stress, Jimei DPC applies high-vacuum magnetron sputtered titanium/copper atomic adhesion layers. This achieves chemical and mechanical bond strength >30 N/cm, withstanding over 100,000 hot/cold thermal cycles without delamination.
