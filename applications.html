Electronic systems operate under extreme physical constraints: high voltage breakdown, localized heat fluxes exceeding 500 W/cm², millimeter-wave dielectric loss, and hermetic isolation in corrosive environments. Jimei Materials manufactures custom ceramic substrates configured to resolve these exact failure mechanisms across six primary industry sectors.
