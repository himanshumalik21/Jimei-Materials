High-Voltage Thermal Management

Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) Architecture for 800V EV Inverters

Next-generation 800V electric vehicle traction inverters require power semiconductor modules operating at junction temperatures up to 175°C with fast-switching Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFETs. Under high di/dt and dv/dt switching transients, conventional insulated metal substrates (IMS) fail due to polymer dielectric breakdown and poor thermal conductivity (≤3 W/m·K).

Jimei DBC substrates bond pure Oxygen-Free High-Conductivity (OFHC) copper foil (0.20 mm to 0.50 mm) directly to high-purity alumina (Al₂O₃) or aluminum nitride (AlN) at 1065°C using a high-temperature eutectic melt. This creates a solid metallurgical bond with peel strength exceeding 40 to 50 N/cm, providing direct heat conduction and dielectric breakdown strength >15 kV/mm.

Heavy Copper Foils (up to 0.50 mm)

Heavy copper foils (0.20 mm, 0.25 mm, 0.30 mm, 0.40 mm, 0.50 mm) bonded to both sides of the ceramic. Balances thermal expansion stress across the core while delivering high current carrying capacity.

Etched Stress-Relief Dimples

Specialized etched dimple geometry along perimeter copper edges mitigates edge-stress concentrations caused by the CTE mismatch between copper (17 ppm/K) and ceramic (4.5–7 ppm/K), extending thermal cycling life.

High Partial Discharge Resistance

Dense ceramic matrices eliminate internal micro-voids, ensuring partial discharge extinction voltages well above 800V automotive DC bus ratings, preventing long-term dielectric breakdown.

DFM Design Rules for Heavy Copper DBC

  • Copper Pullback Distance: Keep copper edges ≥0.50 mm inside ceramic border to maintain creepage and clearance distances.
  • Symmetrical Copper Layout: Balance top and bottom copper volume within ±15% to prevent substrate warping during thermal excursions.
  • Isolation Groove Width: Design isolation grooves ≥0.40 mm wide for 0.30 mm copper to ensure complete chemical etching.
  • Stress-Relief Dimples: Apply 0.15 mm radius etched scalloping along long conductor edges.
  • Corner Radiusing: Avoid 90° sharp corners on copper traces; use 45° chamfers or minimum 0.50 mm radii.
  • Laser Profiling: Precision laser singulation tolerance of ±0.05 mm on perimeter boundaries.

Alumina (Al₂O₃) DBC (24 W/m·K) is cost-effective for onboard chargers (OBC) and low-to-medium power modules up to 50 kW. For main EV traction inverters (100 kW to 250 kW+) running high current SiC MOSFETs, Aluminum Nitride (AlN) DBC (≥170 W/m·K) provides a 7x increase in thermal conductivity and matches the CTE of Silicon/SiC (4.5 ppm/K), drastically lowering junction operating temperatures and eliminating thermal fatigue.

Because copper expands roughly three times faster than ceramic during thermal cycling (-40°C to +150°C), peak shear stress concentrates along the straight perimeter edge of the bonded copper foil, eventually causing micro-cracking in the ceramic. Etching a pattern of small dimples or scalloped reliefs along the copper boundary breaks up stress wavefronts, redistributing stress and extending thermal cycling endurance past 1,000 cycles.
