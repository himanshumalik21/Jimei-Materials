Thermal Management in Photonics

Eliminating Wavelength Shift & Facet Overheating in High-Power Lasers

The output wavelength, optical efficiency, and operating lifetime of semiconductor laser diodes depend heavily on active junction temperature. A temperature rise of just 10°C typically shifts emission wavelengths by 3 to 4 nm, severely degrading pump absorption efficiency in fiber lasers and coherent optical communications. In severe cases, junction overheating triggers Catastrophic Optical Mirror Damage (COMD).

Jimei Materials' Aluminum Nitride (AlN) submounts provide thermal conductivity of ≥170 W/m·K paired with a thermal expansion coefficient (4.5 ppm/K) perfectly matched to Gallium Arsenide (GaAs, 5.7 ppm/K) and Indium Phosphide (InP, 4.6 ppm/K) laser dies. This enables direct die attach with zero solder joint creep or mechanical bow.

Pre-Deposited AuSn 80/20 Solder

Vacuum-deposited 80% Gold / 20% Tin eutectic solder pads (3–5 µm thickness) with gold capping layer. Enables fluxless laser die bonding, eliminating chemical contamination on optical output facets.

Wraparound Edge Metallization

Continuous 3D sputtered and electroplated copper along side edges connects top cathode/anode pads directly to bottom ground surfaces, eliminating top-side wire bonds in compact subassemblies.

Optical-Grade Fiducial Marks

High-contrast patterned alignment marks etched with ±5 µm positional accuracy, enabling automated pick-and-place cameras to align laser chips with optical waveguides and collimating lenses.

DFM Guidelines for Laser Submount Layout

  • AuSn Solder Pad Relief: Keep AuSn pad pullback 25–50 µm from the front optical facet edge to prevent molten solder squeeze-out.
  • Wire Bond Pad Geometry: Maintain minimum 0.20 mm × 0.20 mm gold wire bond landing pads adjacent to the die attach area.
  • Packaging Format: Shipped in waffle trays or tape-and-reel under vacuum packaging with nitrogen purge.
  • Gold Capping: 0.05 µm pure gold flash on top of AuSn solder prevents surface oxidation during storage.
  • Reflow Profile: AuSn 80/20 melts sharply at 280°C; recommended peak bonding profile 310°C–320°C in formic acid or forming gas.
  • Edge Chamfers: Diced edges are micro-chamfered to eliminate ceramic burrs during high-speed pick-and-place.
Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Direct answers to common fabrication, material selection, and layout questions.

Using loose solder preforms requires manual handling and creates alignment skew and trapped voids. Wet solder paste requires chemical flux that splatters onto laser optical output mirrors during reflow, causing immediate laser degradation. Jimei's pre-deposited AuSn 80/20 eutectic solder pads provide precision 3–5 µm thickness, 100% fluxless bonding, and void-free thermal interfaces (<5% total voiding).

While Copper-Tungsten (CuW) provides similar CTE and moderate thermal conductivity (180–200 W/m·K), it is an electrical conductor. AlN ceramic is an electrical insulator (>10¹⁴ Ω·cm), which allows engineers to pattern multiple isolated circuit traces, sense resistors, and wire-bond pads on the same submount, enabling far more compact packaging.

Submount flatness and camber are inspected using non-contact optical laser profilometry across 100% of finished lots. We verify that surface bow remains below 0.001 mm/mm (0.1%), ensuring complete planar contact between the semiconductor laser die and the submount surface to eliminate localized thermal hotspots.
