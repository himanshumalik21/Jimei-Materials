Corporate Profile

Precision Ceramic Engineering & Metallization

Founded in 2016, Jimei Materials was established with a clear mission: to provide high-precision, highly reliable ceramic metallization solutions for demanding electronics that standard PCB technology cannot support.

Our primary manufacturing facility is located in the First Industrial Zone, Xinqiao Street, Bao'an District, Shenzhen, with corporate operations in Zhaoqing, Guangdong. We operate Class 1,000 and Class 10,000 cleanrooms equipped for thin-film magnetron sputtering, dry-film photolithography (achieving 20 µm / 20 µm resolution), pattern electroplating, vacuum brazing, high-temperature sintering, and automated flying-probe electrical testing.

Rather than acting as a broker or trading intermediary, Jimei Materials works directly with engineering and procurement teams from power electronics module makers, Tier-1 automotive suppliers, optical & RF component developers, and research universities.
