Company Overview

Pure-Play Ceramic Metallization & Packaging Partner

Established in 2016, Jimei Materials was founded with a singular engineering focus: to develop and manufacture high-precision, high-reliability ceramic substrate and metallization solutions for demanding power, RF/microwave, and semiconductor packaging applications. When standard organic circuit boards reach their thermal, dielectric, and mechanical limits, ceramic substrates provide the critical foundation for thermal dissipation, electrical isolation, and dimensional stability.

Operating as an authentic in-house manufacturing partner rather than an intermediary or broker, Jimei collaborates directly with engineering, R&D, and procurement teams worldwide. We maintain end-to-end process control across our dual facilities in Guangdong, China—guaranteeing direct engineering transparency, responsive DFM iterations, uncompromised quality assurance, and zero broker markups.

Direct Bonded Copper

Technical Competencies

In-House Manufacturing Capabilities

From raw ceramic processing to sub-micron thin film and high-temperature bonding, all critical steps are controlled under one roof.

Thin-Film Sputtering

High-vacuum magnetron sputtering of titanium/copper adhesion layers ensuring exceptional interfacial bond strength (>2.0 N/mm peel strength) without organic adhesives.

Cleanroom Photolithography

Class 1,000 cleanroom dry-film exposure and development achieving ultra-fine circuit trace resolution down to 20 µm line / 20 µm space with steep sidewall profiles.

1065°C Eutectic DBC Bonding

Precision atmosphere-controlled eutectic furnaces bonding 0.2–0.5 mm oxygen-free copper foil directly to Al₂O₃ or AlN ceramics with void rates verified <1.0%.

1500°C–1600°C Monolithic Co-Firing

High-temperature reducing atmosphere sintering for HTCC multilayer packages, co-firing refractory tungsten/molybdenum metallization into a monolithic ceramic structure.

Laser Drilling & Solid Via Filling

High-speed UV and fiber laser drilling down to 50 µm diameter, followed by hermetic electroplated copper via filling for top-to-bottom thermal and electrical interconnects.

Precision CNC & Surface Finishing

Ultrasonic milling, diamond dicing, and automated plating lines delivering wire-bondable ENIG, ENEPIG, and Immersion Silver/Gold finishes.
