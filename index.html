Core Competency & Hero Technology

Direct Plated Copper (DPC) Ceramic Substrates

Where thick-film screening and traditional DBC cannot meet fine geometric tolerances, Jimei's DPC process delivers unmatched micro-circuit precision on high-thermal-conductivity ceramics.

By combining thin-film magnetron sputtering (Titanium adhesion + Copper seed layer) with Class 1,000 photolithography and pattern electroplating, our DPC substrates achieve razor-sharp line/space resolution down to 20 µm / 20 µm, vertical sidewalls, and filled thermal vias.

Core Technology

DPC

Direct Plated Copper

Thin-film sputtering + cleanroom photolithography. Delivers highest feature density and micro-precision.

  • Line/Space: 20/20 µm
  • Copper: 10 – 100+ µm
  • Vias: Filled / Solid PTH
  • Substrates: Al₂O₃, AlN
Explore DPC
Power Electronics

DBC

Direct Bonded Copper

High-temperature (1065°C) eutectic bonding of thick copper foil. High current capacity and thermal dissipation.

  • Line/Space: 150/150 µm
  • Copper: 100 – 500 µm
  • Current: >50A – 150A
  • Substrates: Al₂O₃, AlN
Explore DBC
Multilayer Hermetic

HTCC

High-Temp Co-Fired

1500°C–1600°C co-firing of green tape with refractory metal (W/Mo). Monolithic multilayer routing with cavities for hermetic microelectronics.

  • Structure: Monolithic Multilayer & Cavities
  • Leak Rate: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s
  • Hermeticity: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s (Helium leak)
  • Substrates: Al₂O₃ (90%-99%)

Manufacturing Excellence

In-House Metallization: From Precision Lapping to 100% Tested Substrates

Full control over critical process stages ensures tight tolerance control, fast prototyping turnarounds, and complete batch traceability.

Tour Manufacturing Capabilities
Vacuum Sputtering (PVD)

High-vacuum magnetron deposition of Ti adhesion and Cu seed layers ensures >30-40 MPa bond adhesion without glass frits.

20 µm Cleanroom Lithography

Class 1,000 cleanroom laser direct imaging and exposure lines produce fine-line circuit tracks and RF ground geometries.

Pattern Electroplating

Precision copper plating baths build thick, void-free conductors and fill blind/through thermal vias to maximize heat extraction.

Laser Micro-Machining

UV and CO₂ laser drilling machines produce clean 50 µm micro-vias, cutouts, cavities, and perimeter profiling.

Surface Chemistry (ENIG/ENEPIG)

Automated chemical lines plate Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold, ENEPIG, or Immersion Silver for wire bonding and solder wetting.

100% Electrical & AOI Testing

Flying probe netlist testing verifies 100% electrical continuity and insulation resistance with automated optical defect logging.

Critical Applications Powered by Jimei Ceramic Substrates

Serving semiconductor packaging, automotive electrification, telecommunications, and industrial OEMs worldwide.

Semiconductor Packaging & MCM

DPC and HTCC carrier substrates matching the coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) of Silicon (Si), GaN, and SiC chips to prevent die cracking.

EV Power Modules & Inverters

High-power DBC ceramic substrates handling high current (>150A) and high operating voltages with exceptional thermal dissipation.

RF / Microwave & 5G Base Stations

High-Power Laser Diode Submounts

Aerospace & Harsh Environments
