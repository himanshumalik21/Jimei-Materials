Solving Thermal & Geometric Limits in Microelectronics

Where standard organic PCBs degrade under high thermal flux and traditional DBC cannot meet fine line geometries, Jimei's DPC process delivers sub-20 µm micro-circuit precision on high-thermal ceramics.

By combining thin-film magnetron sputtering (Titanium adhesion + Copper seed layer) with Class 1,000 cleanroom photolithography and pattern electroplating, our DPC substrates achieve razor-sharp line/space resolution down to 20 µm / 20 µm, vertical sidewalls, and solid copper-filled thermal vias on Alumina (Al₂O₃), Aluminum Nitride (AlN), and Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄).

Flagship Technology

DPC

Direct Plated Copper

Thin-film sputtering + cleanroom photolithography. Delivers highest feature density and micro-precision.

  • Line/Space: 20/20 µm
  • Copper: 10 – 105 µm
  • Vias: Filled / Solid PTH
  • Substrates: Al₂O₃, AlN, Si₃N₄
Explore DPC
High-Current / Power Electronics

DBC

Direct Bonded Copper

High-temperature (1065°C) eutectic bonding of heavy copper foil (0.20–0.30 mm). High ampacity for IGBTs, SiC modules & EV inverters.

  • Applications: IGBT, SiC, EV Inverters, OBC
  • Ceramic Base: Al₂O₃ (0.25 – 1.00 mm)
  • Copper Foil: 0.20, 0.25, 0.30 mm
  • Current Ampacity: >50A – 150A+
  • Finishes: OSP or ENIG as required
Explore DBC
Multilayer Hermetic

HTCC

High-Temp Co-Fired

1500°C–1600°C co-firing of green tape with refractory metal (W/Mo). Monolithic multilayer routing with cavities for hermetic microelectronics.

  • Applications: Hermetic, RF & Sensor Packages
  • Structure: Monolithic Multilayer & Cavities
  • Conductors: Tungsten / Molybdenum (W/Mo)
  • Hermeticity: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s (Helium leak)
  • Brazing: Kovar seal rings & metal leads

In-House Manufacturing & Quality Verification Stages

Direct operational control from substrate laser micromachining to PVD thin-film sputtering, Class 1,000 lithography, and automated electrical inspection.

Tour Manufacturing Facility
Vacuum Sputtering (PVD)

High-vacuum magnetron deposition of Ti adhesion and Cu seed layers ensures >30-40 MPa bond adhesion without glass frits.

20 µm Cleanroom Lithography

Class 1,000 cleanroom laser direct imaging and exposure lines produce fine-line circuit tracks and RF ground geometries.

Pattern Electroplating

Precision copper plating baths build thick, void-free conductors and fill blind/through thermal vias to maximize heat extraction.

Laser Micro-Machining

UV and CO₂ laser drilling machines produce clean 50 µm micro-vias, cutouts, cavities, and perimeter profiling.

Surface Chemistry (ENIG/ENEPIG)

Automated chemical lines plate Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold, ENEPIG, or Immersion Silver for wire bonding and solder wetting.

100% Electrical & AOI Testing

Flying probe netlist testing verifies 100% electrical continuity and insulation resistance with automated optical defect logging.

Engineering & Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Common technical, manufacturing, and international logistics questions answered by our engineering team.

Jimei Materials specializes in fine-line ceramic metallization using Direct Plated Copper (DPC) thin-film photolithography. Capabilities include trace width and spacing down to 20µm, solid copper-filled through-wafer vias (≥75µm diameter), and RF circuit layouts optimized up to 40 GHz on Alumina (Al₂O₃), Aluminum Nitride (AlN), and Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄) substrates.

For radar, avionics, and high-frequency RF systems, Aluminum Nitride (AlN) and Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄) substrates fabricated with Direct Plated Copper (DPC) or multilayer High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic (HTCC) provide optimal thermal conductivity (170–200 W/m·K for AlN, 90 W/m·K with high fracture toughness for Si₃N₄) and low dielectric loss at microwave and millimeter-wave frequencies.
