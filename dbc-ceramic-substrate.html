Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) Ceramic Substrates

High Current Ampacity (>50A – 150A+) Heavy Copper Foil 0.20 mm – 0.30 mm Gas-Metal Eutectic Bond (1065°C) Dielectric Isolation >10 kV/mm Al₂O₃ (Alumina) Ceramic Base

Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) is the industry-standard ceramic substrate for high-current power electronics. Manufactured via high-temperature gas-metal eutectic bonding of heavy oxygen-free copper foil (0.20 mm – 0.30 mm) directly onto Al₂O₃ ceramic, DBC delivers high continuous ampacity (>50A to 150A+), high dielectric isolation (>10 kV/mm), and rapid thermal spreading for IGBT power modules, SiC power packages, and EV motor inverters.

Process Science

High-Temperature Eutectic Bonding Process

Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) substrates are manufactured using a high-temperature gas-metal eutectic reaction. High-purity oxygen-free copper (OFC) foil and Al₂O₃ (Alumina) ceramic plates are heated in a controlled nitrogen/oxygen atmosphere furnace to precisely 1065°C – 1083°C.

At this temperature, a liquid Cu-O eutectic melt phase forms at the interface, wetting both the copper foil and ceramic substrate simultaneously. Upon cooling, the copper forms an atomic chemical bond with the ceramic with shear strength exceeding >20 MPa, combining the electrical conductivity of bulk copper with the electrical isolation and mechanical rigidity of ceramic.

Following bonding, circuit layouts are patterned using chemical etching or precision laser ablation, followed by surface plating (electroless nickel/gold or solderable coatings) and 100% optical/electrical verification.

High-Power Deployment

DBC Applications: Built for High-Current Power Electronics

Direct Bonded Copper is the worldwide benchmark substrate for switching high electrical currents under extreme thermal dissipation requirements. Jimei Materials manufactures custom Al₂O₃ DBC substrates engineered for reliability across 8 core power conversion application domains:

IGBT Power Modules

Half-bridge, full-bridge, and six-pack industrial power modules (600V to 1700V+). Heavy copper foil carries continuous switching currents while Al₂O₃ provides high-voltage galvanic isolation to grounded heat sinks.

SiC Power Modules

Wide-bandgap Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFET packages operating at elevated junction temperatures (Tj ≥ 175°C). The 1065°C eutectic bond prevents delamination during rapid thermal cycling and high dV/dt transient events.

EV Traction Inverters

Primary powertrain motor inverters for 400V and 800V battery electric vehicles. Low parasitic inductance layouts with heavy 0.30 mm copper traces handle sustained phase currents exceeding 300A–600A peak.

On-Board Chargers (OBC)

High-voltage automotive auxiliary power stages and DC-DC converters (3.3 kW, 6.6 kW, 11 kW, 22 kW). DBC enables planar magnetic integration, compact packaging, and direct baseplate thermal dissipation.

Industrial Drives & VFDs

Variable frequency drives (VFD), heavy industrial automation, robotic servo motor drives, and CNC spindle controllers enduring continuous start-stop inductive thermal surges and severe vibration.

Renewable Energy Inverters

Utility-scale solar photovoltaic (PV) string/central inverters and wind turbine pitch/yaw converters. DBC delivers 20+ year field durability through high thermal cycling resistance (>1,000 thermal shock cycles).

Power Supplies & UPS

High-density Switch-Mode Power Supplies (SMPS), enterprise data center server rectifiers, telecommunications DC power plants, and uninterruptible power supplies requiring isolated planar power stages.

Welding & Induction Heating

Industrial arc welders, resistance spot-welding inverters, and high-frequency induction heating generators. Thick 0.30 mm copper foil withstands extreme short-circuit surge currents and heavy ampacity (>150A) without necking.

Why Power Electronics Engineers Specify DBC:

Power semiconductor packaging requires balancing continuous current-carrying capacity, high dielectric breakdown, and thermal-mechanical stress. DBC solves this through:

Massive Ampacity: 0.20–0.30 mm solid Cu foil carries continuous loads far beyond thin films.
High Dielectric Isolation: >10 kV/mm Al₂O₃ barrier isolates high voltages from coolers.
Eutectic Atomic Bond: 1065°C melt reaction yields >20 MPa shear strength resistant to delamination.
Matched CTE to Silicon: Alumina base restrains copper expansion, reducing die-attach solder fatigue.
