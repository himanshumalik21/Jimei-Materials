Hermetic Reliability Physics

Monolithic Ceramic Envelopes with Verified Helium Hermeticity

Polymer-sealed and glass-frit housings fail over time due to moisture permeation, adhesive outgassing, and thermal fatigue at glass joints. In downhole drilling tools, chemical exposure and elevated temperatures rapidly degrade standard organic packages, resulting in catastrophic instrument failure.

High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic (HTCC) resolves this by co-sintering 90%–96% alumina green tape layers together with refractory tungsten (W) or molybdenum (Mo) conductor pastes at 1500°C to 1600°C in a reducing atmosphere. The resulting structure is a single solid monolithic block of ceramic with embedded internal circuit traces, ground planes, and cavities. Helium mass spectrometer testing verifies leak rates <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s.

Hermetic Integrity Metrics

Hermetic Leak Rate: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s
Co-Firing Temperature: 1500°C – 1600°C
Metal Brazing Temp: 800°C – 850°C (AgCu)
Internal Metallurgy: Tungsten (W) / Moly (Mo)
External Plating: Ni 2–6 µm / Au 0.5–1.5 µm

Monolithic Multilayer Co-Firing

Ceramic tapes and refractory metallization are sintered simultaneously, eliminating interfacial delamination boundaries and creating single-body monolithic envelopes with embedded routing.

Brazed Kovar Seal Rings & Leads

Kovar (Fe-Ni-Co alloy) seal rings, pins, and lead frames are brazed at 800°C–850°C using silver-copper eutectic alloys, enabling parallel seam welding or laser hermetic lid sealing.

Multi-Tier 3D Cavity Design

Stepped internal cavity tiers support multi-level wire-bonding (die attach on base tier, bond wire pads on intermediate tiers, seal ring on top tier), maximizing packaging volumetric density.

DFM Guidelines for HTCC Multilayer Cavity Packages

  • Cavity Step Width: Design minimum 0.50 mm shelf width for wire-bond bonding tiers to accommodate wedge tools.
  • Shrinkage Compensation: HTCC green tapes shrink 15%–18% during 1550°C sintering; our CAD tooling applies proprietary scaling algorithms.
  • Seal Ring Width: Maintain minimum 0.80 mm Kovar seal ring flange width for reliable parallel seam sealing.
  • Lead Attach Options: Top-brazed gull-wing leads, bottom-brazed pin grid arrays (PGA), or surface mount leadless pads (LCC).
  • Lid Sealing Compatibility: Compatible with gold-tin preform lids or resistance welding of Kovar lids.
  • Lot Qualification: Every delivery includes helium mass spectrometer leak test certification.
Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Direct answers to common fabrication, material selection, and layout questions.

Hermetic packages are pressurized inside a helium bombing chamber at 30 to 60 psi for several hours, followed by transfer to a vacuum test chamber connected to a calibrated helium mass spectrometer. Any helium escaping through microscopic fissures is detected, verifying that the leak rate is strictly below 1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s.

Kovar (an iron-nickel-cobalt alloy) possesses an expansion curve (approx. 5.5 ppm/K) engineered to match high-purity alumina ceramics (6.5–7.5 ppm/K). When brazed together at 800°C–850°C using silver-copper eutectic solder, the joint experiences minimal residual shear stress during cooling, ensuring crack-free hermetic seals over extreme thermal ranges.

Custom HTCC manufacturing requires multi-layer tape casting, CNC via punching, precision screen printing of refractory conductor pastes, high-pressure lamination, high-temperature sintering (1500°C–1600°C), electroplating, and metal brazing. The standard engineering prototype lead time is approximately 6 to 8 weeks.
