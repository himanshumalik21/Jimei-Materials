Verification Protocols

Step-by-Step Quality Inspection Architecture

Every substrate lot passes through comprehensive physical, electrical, and optical inspection stages before release.

1. 100% Electrical Continuity & Isolation Testing

Electrical Integrity

Double-sided flying-probe testers and custom pin-fixture testers inspect 100% of finished circuits against client netlists. Net-to-net continuity is verified at <5Ω resistance, and isolation between adjacent traces is tested at >100 MΩ up to 500V DC. Vias are 100% electrically validated to guarantee zero open circuits or resistive barrels.

2. Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)

Trace & Space Fidelity

High-resolution AOI vision systems compare 100% of panel circuit geometry against CAD/Gerber data. The system automatically flags trace width violations, spacing infringements, mouse-bites, copper protrusions, residual etch bridges, and surface scratches with sub-micron pixel resolution down to 5 µm.

3. 5-Point XRF Plating Thickness Verification

Surface Finish Control

Plating thickness for ENIG (Electroless Nickel / Immersion Gold) and ENEPIG finishes is measured at 5 standardized points across each production panel using calibrated X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry. We ensure nickel (3–7 µm) and gold (0.05–0.20 µm) deposits maintain tight ±1.5 to 2.0 µm tolerances for consistent wire-bonding and soldering.

4. Helium Mass Spectrometer Hermeticity Testing

HTCC Hermetic Seal

High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic (HTCC) packages undergo pressurized helium bombing followed by high-vacuum mass spectrometry detection. We verify hermetic seal leak rates remain strictly below 1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s to guarantee absolute environmental isolation against moisture, gas ingress, and oxidation.

5. Thermal Shock & Thermal Cycling Reliability

Thermal Endurance

Production sample coupons undergo liquid-to-liquid or air-to-air thermal shock cycling between -55°C and +150°C for 500 to 1,000 continuous cycles. Metallographic cross-sections confirm that copper-ceramic interfaces and through-ceramic vias remain free from delamination, micro-cracks, or barrel fracturing.

6. Metallographic Cross-Section & SEM Microanalysis

Microstructure & Vias

Destructive micro-sectioning and Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) inspect copper grain structures, interfacial Ti/Cu seed layer adhesion, and via hole filling. Solid copper filled vias are analyzed to confirm 100% void-free electrodeposition and uniform barrel wall coverage.
