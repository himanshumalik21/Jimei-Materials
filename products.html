Quick Decision Guide

How to Choose: Three Rules of Thumb

Follow these three core criteria to pinpoint your optimal ceramic technology in seconds.

Rule 1: Choose DPC When

  • Circuit lines/spaces are <100 µm (down to 20 µm).
  • You require solid copper-filled vias or thermal vias.
  • High-frequency RF impedance control (up to 40 GHz) is needed.
  • Building laser diode submounts or micro-TEC coolers.
Go to DPC Technology Page →

Rule 2: Choose DBC When

  • Continuous current capacity exceeds 50A to 150A+.
  • Operating voltages are 600V to 1700V+ (IGBT / SiC).
  • Heavy copper foil thickness of 0.20 mm to 0.50 mm is required.
  • Building EV traction inverters, OBCs, or industrial motor drives.
Go to DBC Technology Page →

Rule 3: Choose HTCC When

  • True hermetic cavity sealing (<1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s) is required.
  • You need internal multilayer routing and buried stripline RF feedthroughs.
  • Design requires brazed Kovar seal rings or metal leadframes.
  • Operating in extreme harsh environments (aerospace, sensors).

Flagship Technology Thin Film + Electroplating

Direct Plated Copper (DPC)

Direct Plated Copper combines vacuum magnetron sputtering with cleanroom photolithography (20 µm L/S) and pattern electroplating on Al₂O₃, AlN (≥170 W/m·K), and Si₃N₄ ceramic bases. Ideal for optical transceivers, laser diode submounts, RF circuits up to 40 GHz, and thermoelectric coolers.

Resolution: 20 µm / 20 µm
Copper: 10 µm – 105 µm (150 µm max)
Vias: Laser solid filled (50 µm ⌀)
Finishes: ENIG, ENEPIG, Direct Au
DPC Specifications & DFM Request DPC Quote
High Power Ampacity 1065°C Eutectic Bonding

Direct Bonded Copper (DBC)

Direct Bonded Copper utilizes high-temperature eutectic bonding at 1065°C to attach 0.20 mm to 0.50 mm heavy copper foils directly to alumina (Al₂O₃) ceramic. Engineered for high-voltage isolation (>3 kV) and heavy current (>100A) in IGBT modules, SiC traction inverters, and EV onboard chargers.

Foil Thickness: 0.20, 0.25, 0.30 mm
Ampacity: >50A – 150A+
Isolation: >3,000 VDC
Base: Al₂O₃ (0.25 – 1.0 mm)
DBC Specifications & DFM Request DBC Quote
Monolithic Hermetic 1500°C–1600°C Co-Firing

High-Temp Co-Fired Ceramic (HTCC)

High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic packages feature monolithic multilayer construction co-fired at 1500°C–1600°C with internal tungsten/molybdenum conductors, multi-tier wire-bonding cavities, buried RF feedthroughs, and brazed Kovar seal rings with verified helium leak rates <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s.

Structure: Multilayer & Cavities
Hermeticity: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s
Conductors: Tungsten / Molybdenum
Brazing: Kovar seal rings
HTCC Specifications & DFM Request HTCC Quote

Bare Technical Ceramics

High-purity Alumina (96% and 99.6%), Aluminum Nitride (≥170 W/m·K), Silicon Nitride (high fracture toughness), and Silicon Carbide plates. Complete in-house laser cutting, diamond dicing, CNC ultrasonic milling, and precision lapping/polishing to tight dimensional tolerances.

AlN: 170 – 200 W/m·K
Al₂O₃: 96% and 99.6% purity
Si₃N₄: >650 MPa flexural strength
Machining: Laser dicing & CNC grinding
