Active Metal Brazing on Si₃N₄ & AlN

Vacuum active brazing engineered for SiC MOSFETs and 800V EV traction inverters withstanding >5,000 extreme thermal shock cycles.

Substrates: Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄), AlN Copper: 0.2mm – 0.8mm Cycles: >5,000 (-55°C to +175°C)

HTCC Ceramic Packages

High-Temperature Co-Fired Ceramic

Monolithic 3D ceramic packages co-fired at 1500°C–1600°C with tungsten/molybdenum vias, internal cavities, and hermetic seal rings for aerospace and defense.

Layer Count: Up to 40+ layers
Hermeticity: <1×10⁻⁸ atm·cc/s
Operating Temp: Up to 800°C
Brazing: Kovar rings & leadframes
