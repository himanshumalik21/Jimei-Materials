Thermomechanical Physics

Solving the Silicon-to-Substrate Thermal Expansion Mismatch

In high-performance flip-chip and chip-scale packaging (CSP), the thermal expansion differential between the semiconductor die and the carrier substrate represents the primary root cause of micro-bump solder shearing, underfill delamination, and silicon die cracking. Standard organic laminates (FR-4 / BT resin) exhibit high coefficients of thermal expansion (CTE) of 14 to 17 ppm/K, while silicon operates at 2.6 to 3.2 ppm/K.

Jimei Materials utilizes Aluminum Nitride (AlN) and high-purity Al₂O₃ 99.6% as carrier platforms. AlN provides a near-perfect CTE match of 4.5 ppm/K alongside thermal conductivity of ≥170 W/m·K. This thermal match drastically mitigates shear stress on micro-bumps during thermal cycling, extending package fatigue life under extreme computing loads.

CTE Comparison (ppm/K)

Silicon Die (Si): 2.6 – 3.2
AlN Ceramic (Jimei): 4.5 (Optimal Match)
Alumina 99.6% (Jimei): 7.2 – 7.8
Standard FR-4 PCB: 14.0 – 17.0 (High Risk)

20 µm Fine-Line Lithography

Fabricated in Class 1,000 cleanrooms using optical photolithography and vacuum magnetron sputtering. Enables fine line and space resolution down to 20 µm / 20 µm with line width accuracy of ±5 µm for high-density interconnect (HDI) routing.

Solid Copper Filled Vias

Laser-drilled through-ceramic vias (50 µm to 100 µm diameter) electroplated with void-free solid copper. Ensures low electrical resistance, zero blistering above 350°C, and direct vertical thermal transfer from active silicon hotspots.

Dual Copper Thickness

Proprietary capability to fabricate dual copper thicknesses on the same substrate. Allows sub-100 µm pitch signal traces (18 µm Cu) for high-speed I/O alongside heavy copper pads (105 µm Cu) for core power distribution.

DFM Design Rules for Semiconductor Packaging

  • Metal-to-Edge Margin: Maintain ≥100 µm exclusion from dicing lines (60 µm supported upon review).
  • Via-to-Trace Spacing: Keep minimum 80 µm annular ring spacing from adjacent signal traces.
  • Solid Via Pitch: Recommended minimum via center-to-center pitch of ≥200 µm for thermal arrays.
  • Underfill Compatibility: Plasma-cleaned ceramic surfaces ensure void-free capillary underfill flow.
  • Fiducial Alignment Marks: Standard crosshair and circular fiducials etched with ±5 µm positional tolerance.
  • Array Delivery: Singulated individual dies or multi-up scribed panels with 0.20 mm unit-to-unit spacing.
Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Direct answers to common fabrication, material selection, and layout questions.

While silicon interposers require costly through-silicon via (TSV) fabrication and oxide dielectric isolation, AlN ceramic is intrinsically an electrical insulator (>15 kV/mm dielectric strength) with high thermal conductivity (≥170 W/m·K) and a matching CTE (4.5 ppm/K). Organic interposers, on the other hand, suffer from high thermal resistance (0.2–0.5 W/m·K) and severe CTE mismatch (14–17 ppm/K).

Substrates with poorly electroplated vias can trap chemical residues or air pockets, leading to outgassing and blistering during lead-free solder reflow (>260°C). Jimei Materials employs specialized pulse-periodic electroplating on substrates ≤0.40 mm thick, producing 100% void-free solid copper plugs verified through cross-sectional SEM microanalysis.

Electroless Nickel Electroless Palladium Immersion Gold (ENEPIG) is strongly recommended for fine-pitch micro-bump bonding because the intermediate palladium layer prevents nickel diffusion and eliminates the risk of "black pad" corrosion. For high-power laser and optical chips, direct pre-deposited AuSn 80/20 eutectic solder pads provide fluxless assembly.
