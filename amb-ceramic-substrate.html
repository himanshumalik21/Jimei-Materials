Technology Overview

Why AMB Is Replacing Traditional DBC in Next-Gen Power Modules

Traditional Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) relies on a copper-oxygen eutectic liquid phase formed at 1065°C–1083°C. While effective on Al₂O₃, DBC struggles on non-oxide ceramics like Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄) and suffers copper delamination under rapid thermal cycles caused by high-power-density Silicon Carbide (SiC) chips.

Active Metal Brazing (AMB) solves this constraint by adding active elements (such as Titanium or Zirconium) to an Ag-Cu alloy paste. In a high-vacuum brazing furnace (typically 800°C–900°C), the active titanium reacts directly with the ceramic surface, forming a dense titanium nitride (TiN) reaction layer with extraordinary interfacial bond strength (>40–50 MPa).

AMB Manufacturing Process Flow

  1. 1
    Substrate Pre-Treatment: Precision ultrasonic cleaning and surface activation of Si₃N₄ or AlN ceramic plates.
  2. 2
    Active Braze Alloy Printing: Controlled screen printing of active Ti-Ag-Cu braze paste on bonding surfaces.
  3. 3
    High-Vacuum Brazing: Thermal reaction under 10⁻³ Pa high vacuum at 800°C–900°C forming chemical TiN bond interface.
  4. 4
    Photolithography & Etching: Chemical etching of copper circuit patterns followed by selective chemical stripping of braze residue.
  5. 5
    Surface Finishing & 100% Inspection: ENIG/ENEPIG plating, acoustic microscopy (SAT/SAM) void detection, and laser profiling.

Technology Selection Guide

AMB vs. DBC: When to Specify Which Technology

Direct Bonded Copper remains cost-effective for standard IGBT modules, while Active Metal Brazing is mandatory for high-stress SiC modules.

Active Metal Brazing (AMB)

High Reliability

Recommended for high thermal shock, high vibration, and wide bandgap (SiC/GaN) environments.

  • Ceramic Base: Works on Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄) & AlN
  • Thermal Cycling: 3x to 5x higher endurance than DBC
  • Mechanical Shock: Extremely resistant to micro-cracking
  • Typical Applications: 800V EV traction inverters, SiC MOSFET power bricks, aerospace actuators, high-speed rail converters
Inquire for AMB Prototyping

Direct Bonded Copper (DBC)

Cost-Optimized

Recommended for established industrial applications with moderate thermal cycling requirements.

  • Ceramic Base: Primarily Alumina (Al₂O₃) and AlN
  • Thermal Cycling: ~1,000 cycles (-55°C to +150°C)
  • Cost Efficiency: Lower processing cost in high-volume runs
  • Typical Applications: Standard industrial motor drives, solar inverters, welding equipment, standard IGBT modules
